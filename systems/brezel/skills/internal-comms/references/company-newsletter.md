## Instructions
You are being asked to write a company-wide newsletter update. Summarize the past week or month of the company as a newsletter that the whole company will read. Aim for roughly 10-25 bullet points depending on company size and how much happened. It will typically be sent by email or posted in the team chat, so make it easy to consume there.

Ideally it includes the following attributes:
- Links and references: point to relevant documents, records, announcements from management, and significant things that happened in the company.
- Short and to the point: each bullet should be no longer than ~1-2 sentences.
- Use the "we" form, as you are part of the company. Many bullets should say "we did this" or "we did that".

## Sources
Use what is available, and tell the user if the result would be better with more material:

- Cases and work items: milestones reached, projects completed or started
- The records in this Brezel system (find them with the entity tools): new customers, orders, events, key figures the company is happy to share internally
- Agent memory: recurring newsletter structure, earlier issues, company priorities
- Files or attachments the user provides: announcements from management, meeting minutes, all-hands notes, plans for the upcoming quarter
- External press: articles or mentions of the company (web search / reading web pages)

If little is available, ask the user what they want to cover. In that case you're mostly polishing and fitting their input into this format.

## Sections
Break the update into sections of similar things so the different areas of the company are highlighted well. You might cluster into {operations, sales & customers, team & people}, {projects, customers, internal news}, or {external news, internal news}. Smaller companies need fewer sections.

## Prioritization
Focus on:
- Company-wide impact (not team-specific details)
- Announcements from management
- Major milestones and achievements
- Information that affects most employees
- External recognition or press

Avoid:
- Overly granular team updates (save those for 3Ps)
- Information only relevant to small groups
- Duplicate information already communicated
- Personal data of employees or customers that is not meant for company-wide distribution

## Example Format

📣 Company Announcements

🎯 Progress on Priorities

- Area 2

    - Sub-area 2

🏛️ Leadership Updates
- Post 1
- Post 2

🧵 Social Updates
- Update 1
